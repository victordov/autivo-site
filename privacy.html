Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026

This policy explains how Autivo ("Autivo", "the app"), developed by Victor Dovgaliuc ("we", "us"), handles information. The short version: what you log stays on your iPhone. The app sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, which you can turn off, and purchase records needed to unlock Autivo Plus.

In short
  • We never receive what you log: your cars, entries, notes, amounts, places, documents and photos. We have no servers of our own and cannot see them.
  • The app sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports (first-party analytics, to check that the app works) to PostHog, under a random ID. You can turn them off in Settings › Your data › Share usage statistics.
  • There are no accounts, no advertising and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Your cars, entries, documents and photos are stored on your iPhone. With Autivo Plus you can choose to sync them through your own private iCloud, which we cannot access.
  • Receipts and documents are read on your iPhone and are never uploaded.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple and recorded by RevenueCat under an anonymous ID. We never receive your name, Apple Account or payment details.

Information stored on your device

To do its job, Autivo saves what you enter in the app's private storage on your device:

  • your cars and their details (for example name, make, model, year, number plate, VIN, insurer, policy number, insurance renewal and inspection dates, tank and battery size, notes and a photo of the car);
  • fill-ups and charges, repairs and their items, expenses, trips and reminders;
  • documents and receipt scans you add, and the names you reuse (stations, garages, repair items, categories);
  • your settings, such as units, currency, appearance and tips.

This data does not leave your device through Autivo, except in the cases described below that you choose to use. We have no servers and cannot see it. It is removed when you delete the app. Depending on your device settings, it may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone, which are governed by Apple.

iCloud sync (optional, Autivo Plus)

If you turn on iCloud sync, Autivo stores a copy of your data in the private database of your own iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit, so it appears on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. This private database belongs to your Apple Account: we cannot access, read or recover it. Apple stores and protects it under the iCloud Terms and Conditions and the Apple Privacy Policy. iCloud sync is off unless you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time in Autivo's Settings.

Exchange rates

The app downloads reference exchange rates from the public Frankfurter service (frankfurter.app), which publishes European Central Bank data, in two cases:

  • with Autivo Plus, when you log an amount in a currency other than your home currency (the rate for the entry's date);
  • for everyone, when you change your home currency after logging entries, so the app can offer to convert them at today's rate.

The request contains only the two currency codes (for example "RON" and "EUR") and, for an entry dated in the past, that date. No amounts, entries, identifiers or other personal data are sent. As with any request on the internet, the service can see your device's IP address; we don't operate it and receive nothing from it. Rates are kept on your device so they don't have to be downloaded again. Otherwise, without Plus, you type rates yourself and no request is made.

Receipt and document scanning

When you scan a receipt, invoice or document, the camera image is processed on your device. Text is read with Apple's Vision framework and, on devices that support it, Apple's on-device language model (the Foundation Models framework) to find details such as the place, date, items and total. All of this happens on your iPhone: scans and the text read from them are never uploaded, and neither we nor anyone else receive them. Scans you keep are stored with your data as described above.

Usage statistics and crash reports

Autivo uses first-party analytics: anonymous usage statistics that tell us whether the app works (crashes and errors) and which of its features are used, so we can fix and improve it. They are about the app, not about you: they are never used for advertising, never sold, and never combined with data from other companies' apps or websites, which is what Apple and privacy laws call tracking. PostHog (PostHog Inc.) processes them for us. What is sent:

  • named events, each with only the details listed here:
    • the app was installed, updated, opened or closed, and the first time it was opened on a given day, with the number of days since it was installed;
    • the onboarding steps, and how onboarding ended (a car added, skipped, a backup restored, or the sample garage opened);
    • a car was added, with its fuel type (for example petrol or electric) and whether it is the first;
    • an entry was logged, with its type (fill-up, charge, repair or expense), whether a receipt scan filled it in, and whether it was new or edited;
    • a trip started or ended, with whether a km reading was given and whether it was ended from the Lock Screen;
    • a reminder was marked done, with its kind (for example oil change or tyres);
    • the sample garage was opened or closed, and from where;
    • whether you have Autivo Plus, the Plus screen was opened (from where, and for which feature), and the steps of buying or restoring (which plan, its price and currency, and the result);
  • crash reports and short descriptions of errors (for example "the backup couldn't be saved");
  • technical details: app version, device model, iOS version and language;
  • a random ID created the first time the app is opened, so that events from the same install can be counted together.

What is never sent: anything you type, and the content of what you log, such as car names, makes and models, number plates, VINs, notes, amounts, odometer readings, stations, garages, places, documents, photos or receipt scans, and your location. The statistics are not linked to your name, email or Apple Account, and we don't build a profile of you. As with any internet request, PostHog's servers see your device's IP address; the app tells PostHog not to work out a location from it. The statistics are stored by PostHog in the United States, together with those of our other apps and labelled with the app's name, and kept only as long as they help us keep the apps working. PostHog's handling is described in the PostHog Privacy Policy.

You can turn this off at any time in Settings › Your data › Share usage statistics. When it is off, the app sends no usage statistics or crash reports. We rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), with this switch as your way to object.

In-app purchases and subscriptions

Autivo Plus is sold through the App Store and purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your name, Apple Account, payment card or billing details.

To know whether you have Plus, including after a renewal, a refund or on a new device, the app uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat Inc.), a service that keeps purchase records for us. RevenueCat receives from the app and from Apple: which Autivo Plus product was bought, its price and currency, the purchase, trial, renewal, cancellation and refund dates, Apple's transaction identifiers, the App Store country, an anonymous ID that RevenueCat creates for this install, and technical details (app version, device model, iOS version) and your IP address, as with any internet request. The app does not give RevenueCat any advertising or device identifier. RevenueCat also passes these purchase events to our PostHog project under the same anonymous ID, so we can see, for example, how many free trials become subscriptions. RevenueCat's handling is described in the RevenueCat Privacy Policy. The app also checks purchases on your device with Apple's StoreKit, so Plus keeps working if RevenueCat can't be reached.

You can manage or cancel a subscription in your device's Settings under your name › Subscriptions. Apple's handling of this information is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Permissions

Autivo asks for a permission only when you first use the feature that needs it, and the rest of the app works without it:

  • Camera: to scan documents and receipts.
  • Photos: to pick a photo of your car or import a document. iOS gives Autivo only the photos you pick.
  • Notifications: for the reminders and alerts described below.

Notifications

All notifications are local: they are scheduled on your device, no push server is involved and nothing is sent anywhere. Autivo uses them for the reminders you set up, documents that are about to expire, and, if you start a free trial, a reminder 2 days before the trial ends. You can turn them off in Autivo's Settings or in your device's Settings.

Widgets and Live Activity

Autivo's widgets and the trip Live Activity show a summary of your data, such as this month's spending, what is coming up and the running trip. The app shares this summary with them through a container on your device (an App Group) that only Autivo's own components can read. It never leaves your device. Widgets and Live Activities can be visible on your Lock Screen; you can remove them or turn the Lock Screen timer off for a trip.

Backups and exports

When you create a backup or export a report, Autivo creates the file on your device and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to save or send it, for example Files, iCloud Drive or email. These files contain your data, including attached documents in backups, so keep them somewhere safe.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Delete any entry, car or document in the app and it is removed from your device.
  • Delete the app to remove everything Autivo stores on that device.
  • With iCloud sync on, deletions in the app are synced to your iCloud as well. To remove Autivo's data from iCloud entirely, use iCloud storage management in your device's Settings (your name › iCloud).
  • Backup and export files you created, and your own device backups, are yours to keep or delete.

We hold no copy of what you log, so we cannot recover it for you.

Children

Autivo is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region). The app collects no information that identifies anyone, only the anonymous statistics and purchase records described above.

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR give you rights over personal data that a company holds about you, including access, correction, deletion and objection. What you log never reaches us; you can export it with a backup and delete it at any time in the app. The usage statistics and purchase records are tied only to random IDs, not to your name or Apple Account, so we usually can't tell which records are yours. You can stop the statistics at any time in Settings, and you can write to us at the address below with any request; we will answer within one month.

Changes to this policy

If Autivo's data practices change, for example if a future version adds a feature that needs to collect data, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released, and mention the change in the release notes.
